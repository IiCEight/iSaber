Review notes

  1. A · Vivid: saturated warm/cool fields, filled overlapping circles, oversized sans-serif typography, a dark navigation bar, and quiet off-white article sections. This is the closer direction to the supplied reference.
  2. B · Editorial: paper and sage fields, fine circular outlines with a restrained terracotta accent, serif display/article typography, and a light navigation bar.
  3. Both retain the approved full-screen opening, horizontal top navigation, one shared article model, and the same article content and hierarchy.
  4. All copy and article entries are samples. Fonts use local fallbacks; final font files, licensing, responsive refinement, contrast checks, and animation remain part of the chosen direction's prototype.
  5. Choose the direction first, then refine it. The static circles show a possible composition, not a verified reproduction of the reference's WebGL animation.
