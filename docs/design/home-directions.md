# Home visual directions

Status: The owner approved the full-screen composition/horizontal navigation, selected **A · Vivid** and positively reviewed the [motion prototype](home-prototype.md). The [React integration](home-integration.md) is merged/live. These static studies remain design history.

Open [the comparison](home-directions.html) directly in a browser. Choose **A · Vivid** or **B · Editorial**, then compare Desktop, Tablet, Mobile, and full-screen views. Both studies use the same approved layout and sample content. No installation, external fonts, live article requests, or production changes are required. Without JavaScript, the default Vivid study remains readable; the comparison controls require JavaScript.

## A · Vivid

The closer interpretation of the owner's C2 Montréal screenshot. Warm coral/pink and saturated blue fields occupy the opening canvas. Filled circular forms overlap behind large sans-serif identity typography. A dark horizontal navigation bar anchors the composition. A restrained off-white article section gives long titles and summaries room to breathe after the expressive opening.

Proposed palette: near-black `#121215`, warm coral `#ef623c`, pink `#f75cac`, blue `#2442e6`, and off-white `#f8f5ee`. Colored fields are primarily decorative; headline and control backplates keep essential text separate from busy graphics.

Proposed motion after selection: a coordinated introduction of the circles and typography, followed by restrained article reveals. The static circular study is a composition proposal, not a claim of matching the reference's exact WebGL appearance or movement.

## B · Editorial

An alternative that gives the same full-screen structure a literary character. Paper, sage, and ink colors combine with fine circular outlines and a terracotta accent. Serif display typography carries the identity and article titles; interface labels stay in a simple sans-serif. A light top bar joins the page's paper background.

Proposed palette: paper `#f2eee5`, sage `#d9e0d1`, sand `#e9decb`, ink `#213c32`, and terracotta `#9c4b38`.

Proposed motion after selection: slow geometric movement and a concise typographic entrance, with quieter article transitions. This direction retains the large opening but changes its visual voice.

## Shared constraints

- Full-screen hero; typography and graphics share the canvas.
- Horizontal desktop navigation, compact mobile top menu, and an immediately usable writing link.
- Introduction and recent public articles follow the opening.
- All entries remain articles, organized by categories/tags. No fixed Journal/Technical/Projects tabs.
- English/Chinese samples, optional metadata, and single/empty/unavailable states remain reviewable.
- Current studies use system font fallbacks. Select actual production fonts and verify licensing, Chinese coverage, loading behavior, and layout stability during refinement.
- Reduced motion, keyboard access, responsive behavior, contrast, and agreed performance budgets remain acceptance requirements. Static source checks do not establish browser or accessibility acceptance.

## Selection

Recommended starting direction: **A · Vivid**, because it responds most directly to the reference the owner selected. B provides a meaningful alternative within the same approved layout.

The owner selected A. Its motion prototype is the next review. B remains a reference rather than a second palette or typographic system to mix into the selected design.

These static studies were initially prepared without browser playback. The selected A direction now has a separate [motion prototype and Chromium verification record](home-prototype.md). The owner positively reviewed that motion; live integrated-site refinement remains open.
